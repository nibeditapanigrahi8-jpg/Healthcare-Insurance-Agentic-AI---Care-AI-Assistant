CAREPLUS HEALTH INSURANCE
MEMBER BENEFITS AND COVERAGE GUIDE

Document Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026


1. PHYSICAL THERAPY

Physical therapy is a covered service under eligible CarePlus plans.

Members may receive physical therapy when the service is medically necessary
and provided by an eligible healthcare provider.

For HMO plans, a referral may be required before receiving specialist services
or certain covered services.

For PPO plans, referrals are generally not required for specialist services.

Coverage is subject to the member's specific plan benefits, deductible,
copayment, coinsurance, and applicable limitations.


2. MRI SERVICES

MRI scans may require prior authorization depending on the member's plan,
the type of service, and the clinical circumstances.

Providers should submit the required clinical information to CarePlus for
authorization review.

Members should verify authorization requirements before receiving
non-emergency MRI services.

Emergency services are handled according to applicable emergency-care
provisions.


3. REFERRALS

HMO members may be required to obtain a referral from their primary care
provider before receiving certain specialist services.

PPO members generally do not require referrals to see specialists.

Referral requirements may vary depending on the member's specific plan
and the healthcare service.


4. EMERGENCY SERVICES

Emergency services should not be delayed while waiting for prior authorization.

Members should seek emergency medical care when they believe they have
a medical emergency.

Emergency claims are processed according to the member's plan benefits
and applicable emergency-care rules.


5. OUT-OF-NETWORK SERVICES

PPO plans may provide coverage for eligible out-of-network services,
although members may have higher cost-sharing responsibilities.

HMO plans generally provide limited or no coverage for routine
out-of-network services except where required by the plan or applicable rules.

Members should verify network status before receiving non-emergency care.


6. CLAIMS

Members should retain relevant medical bills and documentation associated
with healthcare services.

Claims must be submitted according to CarePlus claims procedures and
applicable submission deadlines.

Claim processing depends on the information received and the member's
plan benefits.


7. APPEALS

Members may have the right to appeal certain coverage or claim decisions.

An appeal should include relevant supporting documentation.

Appeals are reviewed according to applicable CarePlus procedures and
member-plan provisions.
